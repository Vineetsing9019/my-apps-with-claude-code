How to play

  1. Invite a friend who is online. When they accept you're both in a room (up to 6 singers).
  2. The host taps Start. Everyone gets a turn, one at a time, like carrom.
  3. On your turn your mic goes live and everyone else's is muted. Sing a song that starts with the letter in the middle.
  4. Tap Done and type the last word you sang. Its last letter goes to the next singer.
  5. Pass or run out of time and you lose a ❤. Last singer standing wins!
